Create a new project: music sheet app for violin. This will include annotation editing, sheet fixing, automatic page flipping, and built-in metronome. I will be using the app on an Android Samsung tablet.

# Features
## 1. Annotation Editing
Samsung S pen can be used to edit default annotations in the imported sheet music, such as:
- cutting and adding ties/slurs,
- adding fingerings,
- erasing and adding bowing marks,
- adding sharps/flats (may require moving the notes or even bar lines to make for extra space)
- adding note notations such as tenuto and fermata.
I want these edits to be displayed similar to how it's printed, not my raw handwritten annotations. For that, my raw handwritten notations need to be converted, and a small local AI may be used if deemed necessary. These edits are all activated under a single mode for all types of edits mentioned above.
Other standard options of annotations should be available as well but is kept as the raw annotation:
- Pen with adjustable sizes and colour (no need for special pen styles like fountain pen).
- Eraser with adjustable size and the two modes: area and stroke.
- Highlighter with adjustable size and colors.

## 2. Sheet fixing
The sheet music imported may not be straight and the printed areas may be discoloured due to the scanning process.
The app shall include a feature to apply perspective correction and mesh warp to straighten the music sheet pages, as well as filling up the discoloured sheet.

## 3. Automatic Page Flipping
A microphone is used to listen to the music. From there, the app decides when to flip the page. Music could come from more instrument types other than the violin as I'm playing in the orchestra.
A custom/off-the-shelve device could also be connected via Bluetooth. The device would be able to send signals which turns the page left or right.

## 4. Metronome
Built-in metronome
